Relay

A drop-in contact and feedback form. Visitors fill a short form (every field optional, but checked when filled) and it opens WhatsApp or email with the message ready to send. No server, no sign-up.

Use it

<script src="https://ritesh-dhekane.github.io/relay/v1/relay.js" defer></script>

<!-- A form on the page -->
<relay-form source="my-app" kind="feedback" fields="name,email,message"
  whatsapp="91XXXXXXXXXX" email="you@example.com"></relay-form>

<!-- Or a floating button that opens it in a pop-up -->
<relay-button source="my-app" kind="support" whatsapp="91XXXXXXXXXX"></relay-button>

See the README for every option.
